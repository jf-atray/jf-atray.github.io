"Missy", also known as Maury Peggy, was a 2024 to 2026 character. The inspiration, came from a conversation about Star Wars' ugnaughts. You know them as the old wrinkly pig puppets in Empire Strikes Back, in Bespin. I thought about my favorite pig puppet, Miss Piggy.
  {% xterm_file missy/desc_1.txt cap:right %}
After spending the last two years playing Tuco, the engineer, Missy mixed things up to be a "slicer", the computer hackers of Star Wars. I had a better grip this time, that like the films, different Era will fast forward time. So I knew phase-one Missy would be a basement hacker. A lot of horrible things happened in real life that I didn't get to see where this evil story would go at the time.

---

Years later, in the time of the Galactic Empire, Missy found herself much more important, serving in the logistics branch of Czerka Coprporation, developing weapons.
  {% xterm_file missy/desc_2.txt cap:right %}
Additionally, in my art, I'd finally fallen out of love of dark/light/alt stripes. Here you can see that I'm leveraging asymmetry more, both in the shape and the interior color. At this point I was still "shaping" text in-line, and couldn't get as adventerous as I shortly would next era.

---

  {% xterm_file missy/desc_3.txt cap:right %}

Missy didn't get up to a lot Era 3, actually, but I think this is an excellent piece "about" her character, her motivations and her story. I wanted people who'd known her as a figure of trust and authority to do a double take. From all her riches selling weapons, Maury Peggy moved on to a beautique and spa.
